## Design Context

> Updated from current implementation on 2026-10-11. These are design principles, not a claim that every existing screen has passed visual acceptance.

### Users

Chinese-speaking readers discovering short and long-form articles, authors drafting and submitting work, and administrators reviewing content. Public browsing should make reading choices clear; author/reviewer states should explain what can happen next.

### Brand Personality

Editorial, curious, considered. Semi-Overt combines a quiet reading/writing surface with a recognizable, expressive discovery experience. Avoid promotional copy and generic AI-SaaS presentation.

### Current Visual Language

The earlier “subtle motion only” description no longer captures the implementation. The homepage has an eye, orbiting glyphs, a tesseract and layered article cards; category/search include WebGL and list views; desktop home/results/profile have explicit transition choreography.

Keep frosted surfaces, light/dark themes and restrained accents in the application shell. Typography combines the body stack with local Hero Pressure and Zhaohua Display faces defined in fonts.css. Core theme tokens live in theme.css; component motion/layout tokens remain local where appropriate.

### Design Principles

1. Content first: expressive discovery must lead into readable articles and usable writing tools.
2. One dominant action: separate browsing, writing and review priorities rather than making every control equally loud.
3. Purposeful motion: preserve established scene contracts, but do not add long animation to ordinary confirmation/error feedback.
4. Continuity: Sheet reading/editing retains background context; closing it must not replay the underlying page.
5. Plain language: explain draft/review states, save failures and permissions without technical jargon.
6. Real content: decorative home cards must remain noninteractive; do not invent article titles to fill layouts.
7. Device-aware consistency: share typography, color and interaction meaning, not identical desktop geometry. Full desktop choreography starts at 1024px; narrower layouts and reduced-motion paths need separate checks.
8. Honest quality boundaries: frame budgets are not FPS guarantees; desktop screenshots do not establish touch/keyboard/mobile acceptance.
